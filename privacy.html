Privacy Notice

This page explains the current website’s basic handling of information.

Estimate requests

The current website calculates preliminary pricing in your browser. When you choose to email the request, your device opens your email application with the project details prepared for you to review and send to Alpha Galaxy Coatings.

Information you provide

Your request may include your name, phone number, email address, project type, approximate square footage, ZIP code, concrete condition and desired timing.

Contact

Information sent to Alpha Galaxy Coatings is used to respond to your project request and related follow-up. The current form does not itself store the information on this static website.

Future integrations

If the site is later connected to a CRM, messaging service, analytics platform or payment provider, this notice should be updated to reflect those systems before they are used.
